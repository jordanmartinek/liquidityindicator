LIQUIDITY RADAR — ABOUT THIS INDICATOR

Paste this text into the TradingView "About this indicator" box when you publish.


WHAT IT IS

Liquidity Radar maps the resting liquidity on your chart, rates how important each pool is, and shows which level price is most likely heading to next. It also includes an optional Al Brooks microtrendline module for execution and entries. It is an overlay built for intraday futures (NQ, ES, GC, CL) on low timeframes.

Levels are removed the moment they are swept, so the chart only shows live, untapped liquidity.


CORE CONCEPTS

• BSL / SSL — buy-side liquidity rests ABOVE price (old highs, buy stops); sell-side liquidity rests BELOW price (old lows, sell stops).
• Draw on liquidity — the pool price is most likely to gravitate to next. Every level exerts a "pull" that grows with proximity, importance, age, freshness and confluence. The level with the biggest share is flagged 🎯.
• Sweep + reversal (SFP) — a wick pierces a level and the candle closes back: a stop-run, the validity trigger.
• Premium / discount — the upper / lower half of the live dealing range.


QUICK START

1. Add the indicator. Defaults are tuned for intraday.
2. Read the context panel (top-left): TODAY: LONG / SHORT / NEUTRAL and why, plus the market condition (TREND / RANGE / TRANSITION).
3. Find the 🎯 level — the most likely next target. Its % is its share of total liquidity pull.
4. Wait for a sweep (🎯 SFP marker) or price returning into a strong FVG / order block.
5. For entries, turn on ⑬ Brooks microtrendlines and/or ⑨ FVG execution and take only signals that agree with the Daily Bias.


SETTINGS

① Previous-Period Levels — PDH/PDL, PWH/PWL, PMH/PML, daily / weekly / monthly opens and the NY midnight open.

② Sessions — Asia / London / New York highs and lows with session times and timezone. Options: reset each day, remove once swept, relabel unswept session extremes as swings.

③ Structure
• Swing highs/lows (plus optional higher-timeframe swings) kept until swept.
• Equal highs/lows (EQH/EQL) and relative (near) equal highs/lows.
• Fair value gaps, ranked 0-100 (a relative quality ranking, not a probability): gap size, creation volume, displacement and premium/discount, plus bonuses for order-block overlap, inversion, higher-timeframe gap alignment, round number / VWAP / OTE confluence, a nearby liquidity pool and a preceding sweep; discounted when already tested or walled in by opposing structure. Tags: htf · liq · swp · inv · blk. Settings: min strength % to draw, dim or hide weak gaps, max kept, min gap size, optional CE (50%) line, higher-timeframe gaps in scoring.
• Sweep + reversal (SFP) markers with optional volume confirmation and bar-close confirmation.
• Order blocks after displacement, scored 0-100 (min strength filter), and breaker blocks when an order block fails.

④ Countdown — estimated time (or bars) for price to reach each level, from recent velocity; optionally only for levels price is approaching.

⑤ Clustering & Confluence — nearby levels merge into strength-rated zones colored by side (BSL / SSL), each with a 0-100 confluence score. Chart density (Minimal / Balanced / Everything / Custom) keeps the strongest levels bold and fades the rest — nothing is deleted, it just recedes.

⑥ Level Confidence — show each level's confidence % and the ⭐ stacked-agreement flag (the 🎯 target agrees with the draw direction and overlaps an order block or FVG).

⑦ Style — dark or light panel theme, line and label style, colors and FVG opacity.

⑧ Tested Levels — a level a wick has probed but not swept gets a ✓ and a dotted line, and pulls slightly less.

⑨ FVG Execution — off by default. When price returns into a qualifying gap the setup arms and draws entry / stop / TP1 / TP2 with R:R and the nearest liquidity target. Gates: min strength, premium/discount, draw direction, Daily Bias, min R:R. States commit on bar close; setups die on stop breach or expiry.

⑩ Daily Bias — a sticky structural bias from the last two completed daily candles plus the latest PDH/PDL sweep and reclaim. Fast or conservative flip. Can fade counter-bias FVGs and levels.

⑪ Market Condition — one shared classifier: TREND, RANGE or TRANSITION, from efficiency ratio, bar overlap, VWAP-cross frequency and EMA slope (TRANSITION = a trend losing efficiency, especially right after a microtrendline break). Used by the FVG execution gate and the Brooks module.

⑫ Opening Range (ORB) — the opening window (default 09:30-09:35 ET) as a box. ORB ▲ / ORB ▼ marks a confirmed break (close beyond the edge by an ATR buffer for N closes); ORB fail marks a break that closes back inside.

⑬ Brooks Microtrendlines (execution + entries) — toggle "Enable Brooks microtrendline module".
• Microtrendlines from pivots 2-10 bars apart, plus a microchannel detector for strong few-bar channels.
• Break vs failed breakout: a penetration only becomes a genuine break if price does not close back on the trend side within N bars.
• Setups: A failed microtrendline breakout (primary), B break + pullback continuation, C failed-breakout reversal (stricter), D High 2 / Low 2. H1 / H2 / L1 / L2 markers.
• Signal-bar quality and a 0-100 confluence score (A+ ≥ 85, A ≥ 75, B ≥ 65) with configurable weights — a technical rating, not a probability.
• Pending stop-entry beyond the signal bar, stop, T1 / T2 / T3; status moves pending → triggered → stop / target / invalidated / expired.
• Dashboard (top-right) and signal statistics by setup, market condition, time of day and score. Statistics are not a backtest: one position at a time, no costs, stop wins same-bar ties.
• 5m / 15m context uses the last closed higher-timeframe bar; everything confirms on closed candles.


ALERTS

PD level touched · Liquidity swept · Session level touched · SFP fired / long / short · FVG setup approaching / armed / confirmed / invalidated · Daily bias flipped · ORB break · ORB failed break · MT 1-16 (microtrendline detected / break / failed breakout, continuation, reversal, High 2, Low 2, entry, stop, T1, T2). With the Brooks module on, choose "Any alert() function call" for detailed messages (instrument · timeframe · setup · direction · score).


NOTES

• No repaint by default: SFPs, FVG setup states, Brooks signals and Daily Bias commit on closed bars.
• MASTER toggle (⑤) turns all drawings on or off.
• Scores and percentages are heuristic ratings, not probabilities.
